This page is the maintainer’s review desk

It gathers everything on Operations Academia that is waiting to be looked at — job postings crawled from the tracking sheet, candidate profiles filed before the reveal, feedback tickets and site updates awaiting publication. Only the maintainer’s account can read any of it.

📋 Job postings to review

Every job posting waiting for your look — nothing shown. Auto-crawled postings come from the job market tracking sheet and are held back until you approve them; correct anything that is wrong first — your edits are kept and re-applied every time the sheet is re-read. User-added postings were made through the site’s own form and are already live; open one to correct it, or mark it reviewed to take it off the list.

📤 Posted through the site

Candidate profiles people have filed through the site, newest first — nothing to look at. A profile is held until the reveal date, so this is the one place to read a new one; job postings made through the site are reviewed under “Job postings to review” above. Open one to correct it, or mark it reviewed to take it off the list.

🎓 Candidate profiles

Every profile filed through the site, including the ones held back until the reveal. Edit opens the same form the candidate used; nothing here is visible to anyone but you until the reveal date publishes it.

📅 Market year to check

A posting belongs to the season its apply-by date falls in — the final one, or the suggested one where the search is open-ended, and only then the date it was posted. These were filed under an earlier season than their own dates name, which usually means they were posted before the roll for a search that closes after it. Nothing has been moved, and nothing is hidden: a posting flagged here is listed under both seasons already, because the two overlap — so this is a disagreement to read, not a posting to rescue. A posting’s season is part of its address on this site, so re-filing one is yours to decide: correct it in the tracking workbook and it leaves this list at the next build, or open it here to edit or take it down. If it is filed correctly as it stands — a September search that simply stays open past the roll — press Reviewed — leave it here and it moves into the settled list below. It comes back only if its dates change, because then it is a different disagreement and you have not read it.

👥 Registered users

Every registered account — seeded daily from Firebase Auth and kept current by each sign-in: the name they show themselves under, the address they sign in with, and where their conversation with you stands. Tick the people you want to reach and write once: the message appears in their own area on this site under “Messages”, where they can reply to you. No e-mail is sent — the address links open your own mail client when e-mail is what you want.

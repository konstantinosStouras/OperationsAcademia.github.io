Everything waiting for your review, in one place

This page is the maintainer’s review desk

It gathers everything on Operations Academia that is waiting to be looked at — job postings crawled from the tracking sheet, candidate profiles filed before the reveal, feedback tickets and site updates awaiting publication. Only the maintainer’s account can read any of it.

📋 Job postings to review

Every job posting waiting for your look — nothing shown. Auto-crawled postings come from the job market tracking sheet and are held back until you approve them; correct anything that is wrong first — your edits are kept and re-applied every time the sheet is re-read. User-added postings were made through the site’s own form and are already live; open one to correct it, or mark it reviewed to take it off the list.

📤 Posted through the site

Candidate profiles people have filed through the site, newest first — nothing to look at. A profile is held until the reveal date, so this is the one place to read a new one; job postings made through the site are reviewed under “Job postings to review” above. Open one to correct it, or mark it reviewed to take it off the list.

🎓 Candidate profiles

Every profile filed through the site, including the ones held back until the reveal. Edit opens the same form the candidate used; nothing here is visible to anyone but you until the reveal date publishes it.

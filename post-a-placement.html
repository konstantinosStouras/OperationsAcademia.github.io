Report a placement

Congratulations on your placement! Reporting it is free and takes a minute. It appears on the confirmed placements page automatically, normally within an hour — where it encourages your peers still on the market and shows hiring committees where this year's candidates are going.

You need an Operations Academia account to report one, so that we know where a report comes from and can reach you about it. You can correct or withdraw it later from the placements page while signed in. Nothing you enter here is shared with anyone; only the fields marked shown publicly ever appear on the site.

Submissions are not switched on yet. This preview of the new site is not connected to its database, so the form below cannot be sent. In the meantime, please e-mail the details to operationsacademia@gmail.com and we will post the placement for you.

New site preview

This is a preview, not the live site. It sits at /v2/, is marked noindex, and changes nothing on operationsacademia.org. The pages below are rebuilt; everything else in the menu still points at the live site.

What is being replaced, and why:

  • The job postings list was rendered by Awesome Tables, a paid third-party service reading a Google Sheet. It is now rendered by the site itself from a data file held in this repository.
  • Job postings were submitted through a Google Form. They are now submitted on the site, tied to an account, so a poster can correct or withdraw a posting.
  • There were no accounts, no e-mail alerts and no feedback channel that closed the loop. There are now.

Rebuilt pages

  • Job postings

    The replacement for the Awesome Table. Same seven filters, same cards, same Featured badge — plus filter state in the address bar, so a filtered view can be bookmarked and shared.

  • Post a job

    The replacement for the Google Form. Needs an account; the posting appears on the list automatically at the next scheduled update.

  • E-mail alerts

    Subscribe to new postings — all of them, or matching filters — and to changes on the site. With a live preview of the e-mail you would get.

  • Feedback

    Report a problem with screenshots and get a ticket number. When it is fixed, you are e-mailed what was done.

Sign-in, posting, alerts and feedback are inert until a Firebase project is configured. Each page says so plainly rather than failing; the job postings list works regardless, because it reads a static file.

What is left to do, and how the cutover works, is in v2/_PLAN.md in the repository.
